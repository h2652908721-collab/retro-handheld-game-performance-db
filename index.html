The 4 tiers, in plain English

Tier 1 · ~$60-70 — GBA, SNES, Genesis, PS1. Tiny, cheap, brilliant for 2D. Stops at PS1.
Tier 2 · ~$90-160 — adds most N64, PSP, Dreamcast, DS. The "everything retro" sweet spot.
Tier 3 · ~$220-330 — GameCube, PS2, Wii, Saturn. Android, bigger, hotter.
Tier 4 · ~$400+ — Switch, PS3, Wii U. Steam Deck / Windows handhelds. Does it all, costs the most.

Common questions

Why not just buy the most powerful one?

If you only play PS1 and GBA, a $60 handheld gives a better pocketable experience than a $400 one — lighter, longer battery, instant boot. Power you don't use is money wasted. Match the tier to what you actually play.

Are these official performance numbers?

No. There's no official cross-console emulation rating. This reflects hands-on community consensus (r/SBCGaming, retro handheld reviewers). It's a buying compass, not a guarantee for every single game.

What about Switch / PS2 on a cheap handheld?

Be skeptical of listings claiming a $90 device "plays PS2/Switch." It may boot a few light titles at low settings, but full-speed PS2/GameCube/Switch realistically needs Tier 3-4. That gap is exactly what burns first-time buyers.
